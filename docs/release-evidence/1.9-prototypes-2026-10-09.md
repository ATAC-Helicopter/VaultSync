## Implemented boundaries

- The in-memory page coordinator resolves before committing navigation, retains
  bounded back/forward history, preserves history on failed or cancelled
  resolution, and prevents older pending requests from overwriting newer intent.
  A successful new branch clears forward history. Unsupported requests remain
  explicit; repeated navigation does not duplicate history.
- The read-only single-observation classifier binds subject, generation and
  scope, rejects invalid provenance/chronology and reports freshness independently
  of checker support, execution outcome and measurement basis. It preserves
  historical records. Simulations, confirmations and unsupported checks cannot
  satisfy its current-passed-measurement property.
- The existing recovery-confidence evaluator fixes future-dated passed
  verification and restore-drill results incorrectly being treated as fresh.
  Existing warning codes request renewed checks; backup metadata remains unchanged.

The first two components are review prototypes, not integrated desktop features.
They have no device access, UI wiring, serialization or persistence. There is no
resource resolver, observation conflict selection, producer trust check,
dependency graph or overall recovery-readiness evaluator in these prototypes.
The confidence bug fix affects the existing evaluator, with deterministic
regressions rather than measured desktop qualification.
