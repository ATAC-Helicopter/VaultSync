## Delivered scope

The [wire contract](../DISK_IMAGE_RAW_WIRE_1.9.md) fixes exact first-profile
encoding, endianness, source-kind distinction, resource limits and compatibility.
The new read-only verifier snapshots bounded metadata, verifies the complete raw
payload and completion/root, then checks actual GPT against every recorded layout
observation. A forged disk GUID with recomputed unkeyed hashes still refuses.
Generic schema 1 and kind-1 fixture readers/writers retain their old bytes.

The first profile admits at most 8 TiB, 16 MiB **total metadata** including
88 framing bytes, and exactly 128 GPT entry slots. The general manifest/fixture
limits remain unchanged. Oversized metadata is refused from its header, before
allocating its declared body; invalid metadata is refused before payload reads.
Only success exposes an observed image. I/O/permission failure has a typed outcome
without raw exception details; caller cancellation propagates.

This is a Core stream verifier: no application UI strings, native device access,
paths, writes, producer authentication, consistency lease or restore authority.
No physical source was captured by the synthetic source-kind-2 fixtures.
