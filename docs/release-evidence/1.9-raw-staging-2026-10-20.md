## Delivered

Bound and own input metadata; verify original raw bytes/GPT before allocation;
copy into reserved private ext4 storage; verify against the original snapshot.
Seal files to 0400, flush their metadata, reopen matching inodes with O_RDONLY
and close the transaction's writable descriptors. Verify those sealed streams
again, then flush and publish with rename-no-replace before returning an owned
staging session. No public mutable stream, path or handle is exposed. The session
outlives the caller's original streams and is integrity evidence only.
