## Sidebar defect

The maintainer identified the destination cards at the bottom left. Their
`StoredBytesText` came from quota plans computed from the list supplied to
`BackupsViewModel.LoadFromBackups`. Background cache loads keep at most five
backups per project; the full Backups page reloads complete history. Consequently
the same recorded destination could show a smaller total during cached navigation.
Those cards sum `Backup.TotalBytes`, which is logical source size, not physical
allocation or compressed archive size. Devices with different imported histories
can also retain different known totals.

A complete narrow database projection now selects only ID, creation time, logical
bytes, destination path and protection status. It feeds quota planning off the UI
thread during both full and lightweight reloads. The independent plans are cached
alongside page data and update the sidebar even while another page is active.
Partial or empty page lists cannot replace those totals. All 19 locales explicitly
label known logical backup size with the quantity first, and truncated labels expose their complete text
in a tooltip. Language changes reapply cached quota labels without changing data.

Two focused regressions pass. A database with eight 100-byte backups at one
destination plus a separate 9,000-byte backup reproduces the partial-list mismatch
and verifies the complete 800-byte total, protected exclusions and full-plan parity.
A view-model regression verifies that partial and empty page loads cannot overwrite
an already supplied complete sidebar total. Log:
`/tmp/vaultsync-display-audit/sidebar-tests.log`.

## Linux tray investigation

The reported symptom occurs during PC login: two icons control one app, and
quitting one ends the app. On the inspected session, PID 4160 is the only installed
VaultSync process. The current watcher reports one VaultSync item,
`org.kde.StatusNotifierItem-4160-0`; this observation after startup does not
establish what the maintainer saw during login or prove a duplicate-icon repair.

The installed dependency manifest declares Avalonia 12.1.2; the preparation source
already uses coordinated 12.1.4 packages under VS-1994/#776. The upstream 12.1.4
D-Bus implementation requests its service name before exporting the item, retains
its identity across hides and guards asynchronous name release/registration. Its
comments explicitly describe a scanning host adding a second item when it sees
the object before the service name is owned. These changes are absent in the
12.1.2 source. Sources:
[12.1.2 implementation](https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia/blob/12.1.2/src/Avalonia.FreeDesktop/DBusTrayIconImpl.cs),
[12.1.4 implementation](https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia/blob/12.1.4/src/Avalonia.FreeDesktop/DBusTrayIconImpl.cs).
This supports a dependency explanation, not a reproduced fresh-login diagnosis.
