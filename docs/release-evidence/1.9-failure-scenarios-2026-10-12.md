## Implemented boundary

The read-only prototype models one explicitly identified failure domain against
the existing observation/dependency baseline. Exact subject/generation bindings
declare opaque domain memberships; paths, offsite labels and provider names
never establish independence. Multiple memberships are supported, with a bound
of 1,024 records; empty identities, unsupported kinds, duplicate records,
unknown subjects and changed generations reject input.

The result distinguishes incomplete baseline evidence, known modeled coverage
loss, unknown correlation and retained modeled coverage. Copies sharing the
failed disk/account are removed together. An independent payload mirror does
not substitute for a lost key. Missing or explicitly unknown membership remains
uncertain. A conservative pass excludes unknown memberships; a complete known
surviving alternative can retain modeled coverage while unused unknown subjects
remain reported. Disconnected graph subjects do not affect the requested root.

Every conclusion is `Simulated`. Historical observation objects and measured
outcomes remain unchanged, with immutable copied result lists. The complete
graph and all bindings are validated before a conclusion, even for unselected
alternatives. This prototype has no domain discovery/attestation, serialization,
device access, credentials, writes, UI/CLI integration or measured recovery.
Membership provenance/freshness, observation conflict selection, multi-domain
scenarios, stable formats and joint architecture approval remain open.
