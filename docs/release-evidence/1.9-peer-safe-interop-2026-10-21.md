## Correction

Remove raw descriptor extraction and manual reference counting. An owned
close-on-exec epoll monitor registers the socket-bound pidfd. epoll_ctl takes both
SafeHandles and epoll_wait takes the monitor SafeHandle, giving native calls
automatic lifetime protection. The registered pidfd remains rooted through the
wait; synchronized binding disposal still closes the monitor and pidfd once.
Both descriptors require FD_CLOEXEC. Refused acquisition releases both. No PID
reopen, process data access, signal, application-byte consumption or operation
authority is introduced. The Linux x64 event ABI is explicitly packed to 12 bytes,
with one event and a zero-timeout readiness wait.

The unchanged [peer contract](../DISK_WORKER_1.9.md#kernel-peer-binding-prerequisite--2026-10-21)
limits still apply: connect-time identity is not current credentials after
transition, per-message origin, trusted executable/build, human consent, privilege
or global offline/device-write proof. Existing inventory pipes are not connected
to this internal primitive. No rule, quality threshold or exclusion changes.
