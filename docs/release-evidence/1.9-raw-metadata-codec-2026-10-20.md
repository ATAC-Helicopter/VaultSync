## Implemented

The pure encoder implements the already fixed
[raw reader wire contract](../DISK_IMAGE_RAW_WIRE_1.9.md). It validates nonempty
IDs, positive generation, lowercase SHA-256, source kind, tool/time, geometry,
capacity and partition claims before serialization. Its partition copy is
bounded to 129 observations, then checked against the 128-slot policy; caller
collections are not retained. It emits header, source/layout, raw map and
completion/root bytes deterministically, with no filesystem/device access.

Both 512/4096 outputs equal the independently generated permanent bytes exactly.
Invalid claims yield neither metadata nor a root. The inclusive 8 TiB encoder
boundary is tested as a serialization claim: verifying it against the actual
short fixture payload still refuses, rather than inventing acquisition evidence.
The old temporary fixture writer and its 1 GiB safety cap remain unchanged.
