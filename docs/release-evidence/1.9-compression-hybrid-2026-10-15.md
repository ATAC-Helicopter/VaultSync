## Work and safety bounds

The sampler inspects at most 8,192 bytes and chooses one family. Numeric uses
at most two policies; hex one. Original raw/LZ/Huffman costs always compete with
complete transformed framing. Hex spellings preserve arbitrary casing, literal
NULs, whitespace and binary bytes. The reader follows explicit mode 4, never
reclassifies input, and forbids nested transforms. It enforces 128 KiB bounds,
canonical case masks/tags, exact consumption and content/frame hashes. These
hashes detect corruption, not authenticity. No paths, disks or archives are opened.

not qualify production throughput, memory or restore performance. Generated
GUID/counter fixtures are favorable; held-out licensed projects, mixed-language
content, sampling misses, an independent native reader and differential fuzzing
remain open. No worldwide novelty, universal ratio or production readiness claim.
